# Notes

## User preferences

- **Generalist, not builder.** No code, no math. Wants concepts, vocabulary, and the
  judgment to read news/pitches critically. Success = sharp conversation + hype radar.
- Wants provenance. Cite claims to regulators/standard-setters over vendors or blogs.

## Teaching notes

- The core skill of this course is *classification under uncertainty*: take a vague claim
  and sort it. Lessons should end with the user able to place real-world headlines, not
  recite definitions.
- The single most load-bearing distinction: **predictive ML (old, tabular, works)** vs
  **generative AI / LLMs (new, language, hallucinates)**. Most confusion and most hype
  collapses once the learner reliably tells these apart. Taught in Lesson 0001.
- Push every new term into `reference/ai-finance-glossary.html` and hold to those
  definitions — the field abuses words ("AI", "algorithm", "model") as marketing.
- Anti-hype is a first-class goal, not a footnote. "AI-washing" is a real, named risk.

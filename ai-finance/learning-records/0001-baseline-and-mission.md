# Mission set: informed generalist, not builder

User chose the **overview / literate** track for "AI in finance" (from four offered: build it, invest, apply-at-work, overview). Goal is to hold a sharp conversation, read news and vendor pitches critically, and separate substance from hype — explicitly *not* to write code or train models.

**Implications:** No code, no math, no quant tooling. Lessons are conceptual and vocabulary-driven, but the target skill is *judgment* — classifying real-world claims, not reciting definitions. Anti-hype ("AI-washing") is a first-class goal. Ground everything in regulators/standard-setters (FSB, GAO, CFTC, OECD), not vendors, because provenance is the antidote to a marketing-saturated field.

**Teaching spine:** the predictive-ML vs generative-AI distinction is load-bearing — most confusion and most hype dissolve once the learner tells these apart. Taught first (Lesson 0001).

**Status:** active. Awaiting evidence from Lesson 0001 — whether the user can sort real headlines into the two kinds, especially handling the "can't sort" pile — before treating the distinction as established and moving to the application tour.
